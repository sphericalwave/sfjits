bioAlchemist for iPhone and Mac.

What it is

A nutrition and hydration tracker for iPhone and Mac. There is no free tier: logging, editing and deleting need bioAlchemist Gold. Without a subscription the app is read-only, and everything you've logged stays viewable.

bioAlchemist Gold

  • Log everything
  • Macros that match your training day
  • Hydration + electrolytes
  • Plan ahead
  • Full history and trends

Gold is sold monthly or annually through Apple. The annual plan starts with a 7-day free trial; the monthly plan has no trial. One subscription covers iPhone and Mac on the same Apple ID. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID's Subscriptions settings.

Requirements

  • iPhone, iOS 17.6 or later
  • Portrait
  • Optional: HealthKit, camera, photo library, notifications

Common stuck points

New phone or Mac, empty log. Your log syncs through your own private iCloud account. Sign in with the same Apple ID and make sure iCloud is turned on, then give it a few minutes on Wi-Fi to sync back.

Can't log or edit. Adding, editing and deleting need bioAlchemist Gold; without it the app is read-only. One subscription covers iPhone and Mac on the same Apple ID. Restore Purchases from the paywall or Settings › Subscription if you already paid.

Barcode miss. Lookups go to Open Food Facts. If the product is not there, add the food by hand in the library.

Health charts empty. Grant bioAlchemist access in the Health app. Revoking it only stops new reads; your bioAlchemist log stays.

Abs vs Crt. Abs is the template in the library. Crt is the instance you logged today. Edit the template if the macros are wrong for every future log; edit the instance if today’s serving was different.
