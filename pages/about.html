About · Aaron

I survived jiu-jitsu. That's the product.

I'm a Brazilian jiu-jitsu black belt and an engineer in Nova Scotia. I've had almost no injuries in my whole career and barely missed any training time. Not because I'm tough. Because taking care of my body was priority number one.

The problem

Jiu-jitsu beats people up.

It's fun, addictive, social and great exercise. It also wrecks a lot of the people who love it. Somebody tells them they're getting older and should slow down, and they take on that limit as their own.

I don't think age is mostly trips around the sun. It's years of putting your body at #24 on the list. Train like age is a choice.

Purple belt, 2020
The fix

Maintenance for your hardware.

Fuel, a short daily durability session, an eye on recovery, and a mind that doesn't hand its status to whoever's knocking it down. I built apps for each of those for myself. The fitWrench Suite is those apps, cleaned up for every grappler who wants to keep rolling for decades.
