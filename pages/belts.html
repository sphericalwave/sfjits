Hardware belts

You're a black belt on the mats. What belt are you at taking care of your body?

Belts are the native language of jiu-jitsu, so the suite uses them for the hardware too. One belt per pillar, earned by consistency, the way you earned the one around your waist.

Fuel belt {% include belt.html rank="blue" stripes=2 label="Fuel belt example: blue" %}

Days logged, protein and water hit, training-day fuel.

Maintenance belt {% include belt.html rank="purple" label="Maintenance belt example: purple" %}

15-minute sessions done, range of motion gained, fewer pain flags.

Engine belt {% include belt.html rank="white" stripes=4 label="Engine belt example: white" %}

Training in the right heart-rate zones, and respecting recovery.

Mind belt {% include belt.html rank="brown" stripes=1 label="Mind belt example: brown" %}

Visualization and mindset practice, done regularly.

The rules

Consistency, not intensity.

  • Earned slowly. Months, not days, like real jiu-jitsu.
  • A missed day slows your stripes. It never takes your belt.
  • Your hardware belt is your lowest pillar belt. The weakest link shows, so you know what to work on.
