Fingers

Finger conditioning

Your fingers spend their life curling closed. Almost nothing loads them the other way until a gi sleeve rips out of your grip. Load them on purpose, a little at a time.

  • A few minutes, two or three times a week
  • Fingertip holds that build up the tissue gradually
