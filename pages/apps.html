The apps

Everything that supports your jiu-jitsu that isn't technique. One subscription is planned to unlock every app, and each app will be usable for free at a basic level.

01 · Fuel

Fuel it

First to launch

fuelLine

Nutrition tracking for people who train. Logging takes seconds.

  • Food and water, with protein and water targets for training days
  • Nutrition games that earn stripes, like water and protein streaks
  • Your Fuel belt, earned by days logged and targets hit

02 · Maintain

Maintain it

First to launch

bioMechanic

A program, not a catalog. One 15-minute durability session a day.

  • Three questions to start: years training, sessions a week, what hurts
  • A Today card with one session. Nothing else to decide.
  • Timed holds, and a reminder when a routine is due
  • Your Maintenance belt, earned by sessions done

Better together

The apps make each other smarter.

  • flow says you're recovered, so today's bioMechanic session goes harder.
  • Mark what hurts in suit, and bioMechanic picks the routine.
  • Potions adds your supplements to fuelLine's vitamin and mineral totals.
  • Log a hard roll, and recovery comes up in bioMechanic and a refuel in fuelLine.

Technique (the software) comes later, as flashcards. The hardware comes first.
